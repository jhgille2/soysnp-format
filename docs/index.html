soysnp-format

Detect and translate allele encodings (Illumina A/B vs forward strand) in soybean SNP array exports — entirely in your browser. Files never leave your computer. Bundled chip tables: SoySNP50K (60,800 SNPs) and BARCSoySNP6K (5,989 SNPs).

1. Load a genotype file

Drop a GenomeStudio Final Report or SNP matrix here, or click to choose a file.
No file loaded.

2. Detect the allele encoding

Likely format:

SNPs examined
with A/B-only evidence (a ‘B’ call seen)
with forward-only evidence (C/G/T seen)
ambiguous (only ‘A’ or no calls)
conflicting (both B and C/G/T seen)
inconsistent with both encodings
